Visiting the website

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA); the domain is resolved via Cloudflare (Cloudflare Inc., USA). When you visit, these providers process technically necessary data such as IP address, time, requested page and browser identifier in order to deliver the pages and prevent abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Transfers to the USA are based on the adequacy decision for the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or on standard contractual clauses.

After you enter the access code, your browser stores a key derived from it locally (localStorage) so you don't have to enter the code on every page. It never leaves your device and can be deleted in your browser settings.

Requesting early access

The Request early access form sends your details to our small forwarding service antrag.layerframe.at (Cloudflare Workers). It stores nothing and forwards the request as an email to our mailbox hello@layerframe.at via the email delivery service Resend (Resend Inc., USA). If that fails, the page prepares an email in your email program instead. We process the details it contains (name, email address and anything you add voluntarily) to decide on access and to send you the invitation (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). The details are kept in the mailbox at our email provider Migadu and in an encrypted list of testers that is kept locally only. We delete them after the Closed Beta ends unless you take part in a further beta.

Contact by email and feedback packages

If you write to us or send a feedback package from LayerFrame, we process the details it contains solely to deal with your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). A feedback package only contains what you selected in the program; project data is never attached. We delete the data as soon as it is no longer needed.

Licence check, updates and error logs

At start-up and then every few hours, LayerFrame sends your code and a random device identifier to license.layerframe.at (Cloudflare Workers) to check validity and limit the number of devices. The device identifier contains no personal details. Your IP address is processed for technical reasons; we do not keep access logs.

To check for updates, LayerFrame downloads the file version.json from the LayerFrame download area on GitHub (GitHub Inc., USA) at start-up. No user data is sent, and the check can be switched off. LayerFrame only uploads an error log when you trigger this in the program; it is stored on upload.layerframe.at and deleted after 30 days at the latest.

Everything else in the program

LayerFrame does not send usage data. Other internet connections only happen through features you use yourself, such as basemaps, web services, open data or the online place search. Anich – LayerFrame AI runs on your PC and sends no data. The privacy policies of those providers apply.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. Write to privacy@layerframe.at. You can lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority (www.dsb.gv.at).
